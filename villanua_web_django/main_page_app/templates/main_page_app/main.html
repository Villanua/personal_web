Hello Everyone!

I'm Nacho Villanúa

Robotics & AI engineer from Málaga, currently based in Madrid. I design and develop intelligent robotic systems that integrate automation, perception, and AI, always seeking solutions that are technically and practically impactful.

My background combines deep technical expertise in robotics and software with strong problem-solving and project leadership skills. I’ve led and implemented full-stack robotics and AI projects—from embedded control and SLAM to Generative AI platforms and semantic search systems.

Professional Experience

In Generative AI, I developed document analysis systems, RAG-based Q&A platforms, and semantic search over SharePoint using GPT, Gemini, LangChain, LangGraph, and multimodal processing. I've integrated Knowledge Graphs into PostgreSQL, performed LLM fine-tuning (LoRA, quantization), and deployed scalable AI solutions via GCP.

I also built a predictive system using neural networks, Monte Carlo simulations, and KNN to forecast public tender outcomes.

I've programmed UR3 and UR10 robotic arms integrated with elevators, conveyors, and different grippers. I deployed and recovered autonomous mobile robots (MIR, Summit XL) with 2D LIDAR and RTK GPS for disinfection and campus surveillance. I implemented localization via EKF, AMCL, and built web interfaces with ROSBridge, SQLite databases for path/action planning, and C++ state machines for mission execution.

I worked with service robots such as Bellabot and Kettybot (2D LIDAR + 3D cameras), and operated Boston Dynamics Spot in events and inspection tasks.

Academic Background

I hold a Bachelor's Degree in Industrial Technologies Engineering (University of Málaga), where I developed a strong foundation in mechanics, structural analysis, electrical engineering, fluid mechanics, thermodynamics, control theory, and electronics. I specialized in automation and embedded systems, learning object-oriented programming (C#, Unity), signal processing, and robotics (industrial and collaborative).

For my Bachelor's Thesis, I developed a ROS-based robotic platform for collaborative surgery. The system included two robotic arms (one autonomous, one teleoperated via haptic interface), force sensors, virtual obstacle generation, and a real-time interface. It supported mode switching via a vision-guided state machine modeled with Markov chains. The project was part of a National Research Plan and published in an international journal.

I completed a Master’s in Robotics and Automation (UC3M), where I deepened my knowledge in mobile robotics, intelligent control, SLAM, 3D computer vision, machine learning, and medical robotics. My thesis focused on LIDAR-based SLAM and segmentation, where I created geometric and topological maps using ICP, NDT, pose graphs, and watershed techniques for door and room detection.
